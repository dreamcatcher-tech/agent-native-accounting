# Agent-native Accounting

A polished, nontransactional **service design preview** for New Zealand sole traders: customer-owned agents, existing Xero books and accountable human review.

The preview uses confident service copy and explicitly illustrative package tariffs so the offer and customer journey can be reviewed as a complete experience. It is not an open accounting practice, a binding offer or evidence of credentials or deployed integrations. No bookings, records, filings or payments are accepted.

## Static site

GitHub Pages publishes `main:/docs` with `.nojekyll`. The site is plain HTML, CSS, a local-only plan walkthrough and a machine-readable JSON profile. No build pipeline, libraries, external fonts, tracking, intake forms or commercial service endpoints are included.

- Year-end: NZ$99/month +GST, clean non-GST sole-trader annual accounts/IR3 preparation and human review.
- Business: NZ$249/month +GST, clean sole-trader Xero, up to six two-monthly GST workpacks plus annual accounts/IR3 preparation and human review.
- Setup: NZ$500 +GST once. Xero separately billed at the actual selected plan.

Annual service is billed monthly; scope and renewal are reviewed annually, and ending early requires reconciliation of delivered work and fees. These are authorised illustrative review tariffs, not actual customer commitments.

The page includes sourced NZ competitor comparisons, labelled illustrative situations, a prominent preview notice and `noindex,nofollow,noarchive` metadata. Noindex is indexing guidance, not privacy or access control. GitHub, as hosting provider, processes visitor connection data under its privacy statement.

Public source only: confidential conversations, launch research, customer data and private notebook records do not belong in this repository. Operating a commercial service or collecting sensitive data requires separately authorised appropriate hosting and service readiness.
