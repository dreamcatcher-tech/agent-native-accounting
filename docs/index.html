A sample answer, not just a report

“Why is profit higher than my bank balance?”

Unpaid invoices can count towards profit before the money reaches your bank. The useful next question is who owes you, and when they’ll pay—not simply what the profit number says you can spend.

Look behind this example ＋
  • Records: sales ledger, unpaid invoices and bank reconciliation.
  • Judgment: check the accounting basis and other cash movements.
  • Open questions: missing receipts and mixed-use expenses stay visible until resolved.

A few useful answers

Less jargon.
More clarity.

Do I need my own agent? ＋

No. Bring your own compatible agent, choose an optional client-side agent, or use a direct human channel. Your agent stays on your side; choosing or changing an accountant doesn’t require leaving your representative behind.

Who checks the work? ＋

Human accountant review is included in both plans. It covers the underlying records, significant judgments and unresolved exceptions—not just a quick glance at an automated summary. Necessary corrections and a human complaints route are not paid upgrades.

Who controls access and approval? ＋

You do. Coordinating records is not permission to sign returns, amend your ledger, pay or file. Keep your ledger and records, revoke access and change provider. Any filing requires separate authority and confirmation of what actually happened.
