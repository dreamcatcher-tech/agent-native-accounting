How it is intended to work

Agents prepare.
People stay responsible.

Automation should remove repetitive work, not the checks or human judgment your finances need.

  1. 01 / Your side

    You, or your agent

    You set the scope and permission to share records. Your agent can coordinate; you remain in control.

  2. 02 / Preparation

    The firm prepares

    Approved records become draft work and focused questions. Important numbers point back to their evidence.

  3. 03 / Review

    An accountant checks

    A named, competent human reviews the underlying records, significant judgments and unresolved exceptions.

  4. 04 / Approval

    You authorise

    You approve the final work. Any filing requires the proper authority, an authorised route and confirmation of its status.

↳

Required review is part of the service. It would never be a premium-only upgrade. Necessary clarification, safety escalation and complaints would have a human route.

Bring your own agent

The intention is to work with a compatible agent that answers to you. Permissions and compatibility would be checked before any exchange of records.

＋

Choose a supplied agent

An optional client-side agent is being considered. It should remain on your side, with clear hosting, costs, export and provider-change terms—not compulsory loyalty to this firm.

○

Use a human channel

No agent should be required to get help. An ordinary human contact route would remain available, with optional advisory meetings scoped separately.

Portability is the intention

Keep your ledger. Retain the records you are entitled to. Revoke access and change accountant without leaving your own agent behind.

A narrow beginning

Start simple.
Do it carefully.

The first proposed pilot would be a completed-period, read-only review—not unattended live accounting.

The intended first fit

  • A New Zealand sole-trader service business
  • Clean, current records in existing Xero
  • Straightforward domestic income and GST
  • A principal available for questions and approval

Xero is the intended starting ledger. No tested integration or partnership is claimed.

Outside the initial scope

  • Payroll, inventory, trusts and complex entities
  • Cross-border tax or multiple currencies
  • Overdue returns, disputes or insolvency
  • Holding money, bank payments or autonomous filing

Complex work would need a separately agreed, competent provider—not an automated guess.

Where things stand

An honest proposal.
Not a launch claim.

The service is in development. Before any engagement, the responsible operator, professional permissions, data safeguards, authority and pricing must be confirmed.

Is the service available now?

No. This page describes a planned New Zealand service. We are not accepting client records, instructions or filings. Please keep your current accounting and filing arrangements in place.

Who would be responsible?

The actual contracting provider and a named competent accountant would need to be identified before an engagement. This proposal makes no claim of existing registration, CA or CPA status, insurance, or an appointed team.

Would my agent approve or file my taxes?

Not by default. An agent could help prepare and coordinate, but permission to share information is not permission to sign, amend records, make payments or file. Human approval and a legitimate filing route would be required.

How would client information be handled?

Data use, model providers, processing locations, retention, access and any subcontractors would need to be explained and approved before records are accepted. Client information would not be an unrestricted training or shared-learning asset.

What would it cost?

No public tariff has been validated. The intended price would reflect the agreed scope, software costs, required review and exception handling. Lower administration is a goal, not a promised saving.

How could I contact the service?

Launch contacts will be added only after the operator is confirmed and the service is ready. This informational page has no intake form, account, booking system or payment facility.
